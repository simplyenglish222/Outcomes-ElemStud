Self-work

3 Your transport

The original group discussion has been rewritten for one learner. Answer aloud first, then add brief notes if useful.

1. What bus routes go near your home? Where do they go? Which stops do you usually get off at?
2. Do you have a bike? What kind? Is it safe to ride a bike where you live? Have you ever fallen off one?
3. Do you have a car? Is it easy to park where you live? Has your car ever broken down? Have you ever rented a car?
4. How often do you fly? When you book a flight, how do you choose the airline? Have you ever missed a flight?
5. Do you take taxis? Why or why not? Have you ever been charged too much or taken on a strange route?
