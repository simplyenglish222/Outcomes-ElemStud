Auxiliary verbs

We use be, do and can as auxiliary verbs to make negatives, questions and some tenses.

Use the same auxiliary verb in the short answer as in the question.

Are you studying? — Yes, I am. / No, I’m not.

Do you work near here? — Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.

Can you see the TV? — Yes, I can. / No, I can’t.

Have to / don’t have to

Use have to / has to for something that is necessary.

Use don’t have to / doesn’t have to for something that is not necessary.

I have to revise for my exams tonight.

We don’t have to get up early because it’s a holiday today.
