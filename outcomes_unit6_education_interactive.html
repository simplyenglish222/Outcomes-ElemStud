Modifiers

Modifiers go before adjectives and change how strong the adjective is.

really / very good = great   •   good   •   quite good   •   not very good = bad

The film was , but the end was disappointing.

Complete with quite, really or not very and the adjective shown.

1

+ good
2

3

+ expensive
4

+ nice
5

+ friendly

+ happy
8

Comparatives

Use a comparative adjective + than to compare two things.

short → shorter; easy → easier; close → closer; hot → hotter; interesting → more interesting.

Irregular: good → better, bad → worse.
